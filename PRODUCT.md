# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Italian B2B and consultative-sales businesses: professional studios, local
service companies, B2B sales teams, and SMBs receiving leads from multiple
uncoordinated channels (forms, DMs, spreadsheets). They land here comparing
Evolv.IA against generic agencies and generic SaaS tools, on desktop, in an
evaluation mindset — skeptical of AI hype, looking for evidence of process and
control before booking a call. The job to be done: decide, in one scroll,
whether Evolv.IA is a serious operator that can be trusted with revenue-critical
plumbing (site, lead capture, qualification, CRM, follow-up).

## Product Purpose

Evolv.IA sells an integrated commercial system — website, AI-qualified lead
generation, CRM, and automations — as both standalone software products and a
unified ecosystem. The homepage (and the shared shell every other page
inherits) exists to make the pitch physically legible: acquisition, qualifica,
CRM and follow-up read as one continuous machine, not a bundle of separate
services. Success is a booked call, driven by credibility rather than
persuasion tricks.

## Brand Personality

Systematic, direct, quietly technical. Confidence over charm. The copy is
short and declarative ("Non insegui i lead. Li governi.") — commanding, not
salesy. The interface should read like instrumentation for a system that is
already working, not like a brochure trying to convince you it might.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing template: hero gradient blob, three icon cards, pastel
  palette, bouncy micro-interactions.
- Startup-hype AI marketing language or visuals ("magic", sparkle icons,
  neon-on-white).
- Consumer-playful tone — this is sold to owners and sales leads who want
  proof of process, not delight for its own sake.

## Design Principles

1. **Read as a control system, not a brochure.** Grid / full-bleed
   composition, glass surfaces, and monospace instrumentation labels should
   make the page feel like it's displaying a live system, echoing the
   product's own promise (governare, non inseguire).
2. **Confidence through restraint.** Dark, glass, low-key motion. No loud
   gradients, no decorative flourish that isn't load-bearing.
3. **One continuous architecture.** Header, footer, buttons, cards and other
   shared shell pieces must look identical across every route, so the site
   itself demonstrates the "unica architettura" the copy already claims
   (see `SiteWideLinks` on every page).
4. **Motion supports comprehension, never distracts.** Moderate intensity,
   clustered around 400ms/1100ms, scroll-triggered reveals, a slow-breathing
   background field — always in service of the CTA, never competing with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on the WebGL background and scroll
  choreography; provide a static DOM fallback.
- Maintain WCAG AA text contrast against the dark background palette.
- Italian-language copy throughout; no accessibility-relevant localization
  gaps introduced by the redesign.
